# Demo artwork and third-party software

The MIT license covers PUP's source code, tools and documentation.

The character artwork, WebP references, original Rive artboards and PUP example
animations in `examples/` are PIYO demo materials. They are supplied for running
and studying this comparison, and are not covered by the code's MIT license.
No permission to reuse the PIYO characters or branding in another product is
granted. Use artwork you own when authoring your own puppets.

The comparison loads **Rive Canvas Advanced 2.42.2**, published by Rive under
its own MIT license. It is a development/demo dependency, not a dependency of
the PUP player. See the upstream
[Rive runtime repository](https://github.com/rive-app/rive-wasm) and
[license](https://github.com/rive-app/rive-wasm/blob/master/LICENSE).

Runtime sizes and animation asset sizes are reported separately. Rive and PUP
have different feature scopes; this is not a general performance benchmark.
